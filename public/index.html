How to play

Crewmates win by finishing all tasks or voting out every Impostor. Impostors win by eliminating crewmates until they match their numbers, or by letting a critical sabotage (Reactor / Oxygen) run out.

Controls

  • WASD / arrows — move (on phones: drag on the left side of the screen)
  • E / Space — use (tasks, consoles, emergency button, repairs)
  • R — report a body · Q — kill · V — vent · F — role ability
  • Tab / M — map (impostors: sabotage map) · Enter — chat · Esc — close panels

Crew roles

  • Engineer — can hide in and travel through vents (limited time).
  • Scientist — checks vitals from anywhere. Doing tasks recharges faster.
  • Tracker — tag a nearby player and see an arrow to them for a while.
  • Noisemaker — when killed, everyone gets an alert pointing to the body.
  • Guardian — after dying, can shield living players from one kill.
  • Detective — examine a body to learn two suspects, one of them the killer.

Impostor roles

  • Shapeshifter — disguise as any other player for a while.
  • Phantom — turn invisible; can't kill while vanished.
  • Viper — victims' bodies dissolve after a few seconds.

Lobbies

The creator is the host 👑. The host can make other players admins 🛡, transfer host, and everyone with admin rights can kick, ban, mute, lock the lobby, edit settings and start or end games. Share the invite link so friends can join.
